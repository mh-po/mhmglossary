# mhmglossary
A mini glossary for Monster Hunter Wilds in Chinese, English and Japanese.

MHWilds Mini Glossary & Notes
This repository is a personal project: a Monster Hunter Wilds glossary and notes database.
It began as my own hunting diary and has grown into a small resource for easier search and reference.

🧭 Purpose
Organize multilingual Monster Hunter terms and notes.

Provide a simple way for players to find and copy useful information.

Learn and practice web development while building something meaningful.

⚙️ About
Built and maintained by one hobbyist player — not a professional developer.

The site is a work in progress, updates may take time.

Some entries may be incomplete or contain mistakes.

I hope you understand, and I wish everyone happy hunting.

💬 Feedback
Please report serious errors or issues that affect fair use.

Vocabulary suggestions are welcome, but updates depend on time and consideration.

I cannot answer general hunting or coding questions.

Feedback (positive or negative) will be read, but replies are not guaranteed.

Leave comments on the Feedback Blogpost.
