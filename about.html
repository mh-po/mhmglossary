‣ 關於本站 (About This Site)

本網站是一個個人業餘開發的查詢工具，旨在方便獵人玩家快速檢索《魔物獵人 荒野》（Monster Hunter Wilds）的遊戲資訊。這並非專業作品，而是出於個人熱情的延伸。資料難免有錯漏，但這正是學習與分享的一部分。
「既然找不到合心水的中文獵蟲搜索器，就自己寫一個吧！」——這就是本站的起點。原本只是我在遊玩時的筆記，後來逐漸整理成網站，把自己想知道卻不易在網上找到的內容收錄下來。
由於經常需要參考外國網站的資料，本站也收錄了多語名詞對照表，並整合了一些小心得，希望能讓大家更輕鬆地享受狩獵。

‣ 若有Feedback、找錯或重要事情要匯報，可以到本站的Blogspot💬留言，謝謝。

‣ 致謝名單 (Credits & Acknowledgments)

衷心感謝所有在網上無私分享狩獵知識與經驗的獵人們！在遊戲過程中，我參考了許多來自 Kiranico, Game8 與 GameWith 以及許多來自專業獵人的YOUTUBE攻略與分享，獲益良多。

此外，本站的大量核心數據源自 LartTyler 的 GitHub 開源專案。在此特別致上最誠摯的謝意。我在部份數據的基礎上進行了重新排版、個人詮釋標註與補充筆記；其餘部分資料則參考遊戲官方線上說明書與遊戲內手冊逐一手動輸入。因涉及大量手動整理，資料庫難免存在些微誤差，敬請見諒。

我並非專業程式設計師或職業玩家，純粹希望打造一個實用的工具網站，讓大家都能享受開心的狩獵時光。本網站主要在 AI 工具的輔助下編寫完成。

‣ About This Site

This website is a personal hobby project, created as a practical tool to help hunters quickly look up information about Monster Hunter Wilds. It is not a professional work, but rather an extension of personal passion. The data may contain mistakes or omissions — learning and sharing is part of the process.

The idea came from a simple thought: “Since I couldn’t find an ideal kinsect quick reference in Chinese, why not try to make one myself?”. What started as personal notes gradually grew into a website, collecting the information I wanted to know while playing but couldn’t easily find online. This project also includes a multilingual glossary and some small insights, aiming to make hunting more enjoyable and accessible.

‣ If you have any feedback, found an error, or need to report something important, please leave a comment on our Blogspot💬. Thank you!

‣ Credits & Acknowledgments

A heartfelt thank you to all the great hunters who share their knowledge and hunting experience online. Throughout my playthroughs, I have referenced extensively from Kiranico, Game8 and GameWith guides and many tutorials on Youtube.

Special thanks to the LartTyler GitHub repository, which served as the foundation for a significant portion of this dataset. I have restructured the data, added contextual notes, and applied custom tags. Other entries were referenced directly from official web manuals and in-game Hunter's Notes, entered manually item by item. As a result, minor discrepancies or inaccuracies may exist.

I am neither a professional developer nor a pro gamer—just a fellow hunter aiming to build a handy tool for better gaming experiences. This website was developed with the assistance of AI tools.
